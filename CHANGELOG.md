# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Overlapping marks through walls no longer flicker, for example walking over a Shortest Path or Path Marker path. They follow one fixed ranking, low to high: NPC tiles, respawns, Path Marker's paths, other plugins' tiles (Shortest Path and the like), your ground marks and tile packs, object marks, Sailing, the hovered tile, hulls, clickboxes and outlines, destination tile, and your own tile on top. 117 HD ordered them by distance to the camera instead, which changed as you moved. Now each rank has its own depth, a few units nearer than the rank below, and every scene object's faces run from the lowest rank to the highest. Marks fill one scene object up to the largest model before a second one is used, and that second one hangs clearly lower so it is drawn first.
- Clear cached Tile Packs and Object Markers settings on profile changes and restart, and refresh plugin lookup on restart, so changes made while HD Tile Markers was disabled take effect.
- Other plugins' tile highlights are drawn in the scene: Ground Items, Fishing, Implings, Cannon, Party (tile pings), Herbiboar, Zalcano, Pest Control, Kourend Library, Blast Mine, Mage Training Arena, Woodcutting and Player Indicators (the tiles under highlighted players, followed while they walk; names and ranks stay 2D), and from the Plugin Hub Motherlode (tictac7x), Stop Misclicking Tiles, Game Tick Information, Clue Details and Spawn Predictor (areas up to 5x5). HD Tile Markers runs their overlays once per frame, as for Shortest Path, so their own settings, colours and distances still decide what is shown; their text, icons, timers and clickboxes stay 2D, as does any polygon that is no exact tile (such as ground items on a table). After repeated errors in such an overlay, that overlay draws itself again.
- Blast Furnace: the conveyor belt and bar dispenser clickboxes are drawn in the scene, with the plugin's colours by bar state.
- Runecraft: the Abyss rift clickboxes, per rift as its options, and Pyramid Plunder: closed container hulls and tomb door and speartrap clickboxes, are drawn in the scene. Pyramid Plunder's own overlay still runs each frame without drawing, so its "hide timer" option keeps working.
- A chat notice when Quest Helper highlights NPCs, objects or ground items as outlines, which cannot be drawn sharp, naming its styles that can. Shown once after the plugin starts, and again when Quest Helper is turned on or its highlight styles change; the new option "Ignore Quest Helper notice" turns it off. Quest Helper's settings are never changed.
- Rooftop Agility Improved (Plugin Hub): its obstacle clickboxes, in its course colours (next, unavailable, stop), and its marks of grace are drawn in the scene, the clickboxes matched to their obstacle and drawn live. Where it highlights an obstacle or mark of grace, the Agility plugin's mark for it is left out, so each shows one highlight instead of both on top of each other.
- Mahogany Homes' furniture and stairs clickboxes, and Quest Helper's NPCs and objects with its hull or clickbox styles, are drawn in the scene. HD Tile Markers runs their overlay and finds which object or NPC each clickbox or hull belongs to (the one whose own clickbox or hull has exactly the same bounds this frame), then draws that one live in the overlay's colours; the plugins still decide what is highlighted, including timeouts and hover colours. Quest Helper's outline style draws past the overlay's graphics and stays its own.
- Quest Helper: its target tiles and its world lines (the line towards your goal) are drawn in the scene; lines are traced back to the ground at both ends and split into pieces that follow the terrain. Its NPC, object and item outlines, arrows and icons stay its own.
- Plugin Hub: Rogues' Den's obstacle clickboxes and hint tiles, and Star Info's star hull (red or green by Mining level), are drawn in the scene; their text, hints and health bar stay 2D. HD Tile Markers follows their rules from their source (credited in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) without referencing their classes.
- Clickboxes of decorations (climbing walls, ledges and other rooftop obstacles, and Object Markers' clickbox style on them) sit on their model again: the scene makes the clickbox from the model's own projection, and a decoration's model sits at an offset from its object that was left out for Agility and Object Markers.
- No more screen-wide square at login: a tile, path or line with a point within about a tile of the camera is not drawn (such points projected to huge positions while the camera came in close), and neither is any such shape larger than three viewports.
- Clickbox borders no longer show brighter, doubled pieces along edges and at corners (Agility shortcuts and obstacles, Object Markers, NPC clickboxes): a step of a fraction of a pixel between two long edges, where the border lay over itself, is replaced by a straight line when that moves no point by more than 0.75 pixel; curves keep their shape. Specks and holes of a few pixels are left out. RuneLite's own clickbox shapes (Rooftop Agility Improved and others) are drawn exactly as before.
- Scene loads: the scene objects' models are kept and reused (the smallest that fits, with room to grow), instead of being made and uploaded again, which stalled the first frames after every load. The Stealing Artefacts, Gauntlet, Blast Furnace, Abyss, Rogues' Den and Star Info marks follow the spawn events a load sends instead of scanning the whole scene each time; Ground Markers' and Object Markers' saved points and Tile Packs stay parsed until their settings change. Boats loading and unloading while sailing are added and removed on their own, without a full reset that turned every mark 2D for a few frames. Changing HD Tile Markers' own settings no longer rebuilds everything. With debug info, the log shows how long each part of a rebuild and of a tick took.
- Per frame: an NPC's clickbox and outline are kept while its projection comes out exactly the same (standing still, camera still); scene objects through walls are pulled towards the camera again only when it moved; carrier normals are set once; polygons that are no tile are not looked for again for ten frames; clickbox rectangles are cut 64 pixels beyond the viewport.
- No flash of thick colour at login or after a teleport: borders are made as wide as wanted for the camera the frame was built with, and when the client then draws with a camera that jumped (more than about four tiles away, zoomed by half or more, or turned by about 30 degrees) HD Tile Markers' scene objects are left out for that frame. Checked from the camera values the renderer is read anyway, without allocating.
- Marks stay in the scene with the camera close by: a clickbox or outline large on screen has thousands of points, and its border and fill outgrew a scene object, so the mark fell back to RuneLite's low-resolution 2D shape. Polygons of more than 256 points are simplified (Douglas-Peucker, from half a pixel up) to 256, which also keeps filling them cheap.
- No more flicker between the scene mark and RuneLite's 2D shape close to a marked object: a mark cut around the player that no longer fits a scene object is drawn whole instead of in 2D, and a model whose faces near the camera leave no clickbox, hull or outline to draw shows nothing that frame rather than RuneLite's 2D shape. With debug info, the log counts shapes left out (too large, out of reach) and cuts skipped.
- Objects partly at or behind the camera (large rooftop obstacles seen from close by) stay in the scene: a single vertex behind the camera used to fail the whole model, and its 2D fallback then drew RuneLite's shape over the scene. Now, as RuneLite does, only the faces with such vertices are left out; vertices within about a tile of the camera too, which projected to huge positions and drew a screen-wide shape for a frame as the camera passed an object.
- Clickboxes no longer wobble: RuneLite's clickbox of a detailed model is the union of one rectangle per face (5 pixels wider on every side), clipped to the model's bounding box, with every vertex rounded to a whole pixel first; each rectangle moved by a pixel on its own as the camera moved. HD Tile Markers now computes that same shape from its float projection, rounded to a quarter pixel and united with RuneLite's scan line (RectangleUnion) whose segment lookup, a walk from the first segment that made it quadratic, is a bitset search here: as fast as RuneLite's own at whole pixels, for every model including unlit static scenery (whose bounding box it takes from the vertices, as Model.getAABB does). The bounding box is taken during projection, so an NPC's animated model is not fetched a second time. Kept for static objects while the camera stands still. The hover colour tests RuneLite's clickbox only with the mouse near the object.
- New option "Player in front of marks" (on by default): with Through walls on, every mark (tiles, clickboxes, hulls, outlines) still goes through every wall and object, but leaves your character uncovered with its exact outline, as Improved Tile Indicators clears the player from its overlay: the player's model is traced each frame, the box around it minus its silhouette is split into convex pieces, and marks keep only what lies outside the silhouette. The silhouette is traced only in frames where a mark lies over the player, and reused while the player and the camera stay exactly the same; marks of more than 600 faces are drawn whole. Depth could not do both: kept behind the player, a mark near it went under the walls around it too.
- The hovered tile is drawn last, over the tiles under it with its own opacity.
- Debug info logs frame times per part (scene, outlines, clickboxes, each captured plugin's overlay, the tick) every five seconds.
- Debug info shows, per plugin whose clickboxes and hulls are identified, how many were found last frame and why the last one was not, and how many Quest Helper and Mahogany Homes marks are in the scene.
- Quest Helper and other identified NPC hulls stay in the scene while the NPC walks: a walking NPC's hull changes every animation frame and can differ by a pixel between two calls, so NPCs are matched with a small tolerance (objects still exactly).
- Shortest Path: its tile index (every scene tile projected, about 10,000 projections) is no longer rebuilt every frame the camera moves. The index of an earlier camera is kept, looked up with a margin and every candidate checked against the current camera; it is rebuilt only when that finds nothing, at most once a frame.
- Clickboxes another plugin drew (Rooftop Agility Improved, Mahogany Homes, Quest Helper) are drawn in the scene with RuneLite's own clickbox shape, so they are exactly that plugin's size; so are walls and decorations with a second model, whose RuneLite clickbox covers both models. Other clickboxes keep the float clickbox, which the bounding box of Model.getAABB (as RuneLite takes it) now bounds again.
- Identified highlights find objects on every level, not only the player's: Rooftop Agility Improved draws a whole course, whose first wall stands on the ground while the player is on a roof, so none of its clickboxes were matched and all stayed 2D (and the Agility plugin's marks were not left out). Candidates whose base lies inside the shape are checked first.
- Identified highlights: an NPC is held from frame to frame only while its anchor stays in about the same place in the shape (a shape could pass to the next NPC when one of several died); a fill drawn before its border keeps the border; shapes that match nothing are not searched again for ten frames while they stay put; tile and hull marks of one NPC have their own keys; a clickbox's bounding box no longer depends on another style of the same model being projected first.
- A clickbox with no polygons left after clipping failed the whole scene route until the plugin was restarted; it is skipped now.
- Clickboxes with a hole (a union of face rectangles enclosing an empty part) draw the hole's border only, as RuneLite leaves it unfilled; it was filled a second time over the rest.
- Nothing is drawn before the first game tick after logging in, when the camera is not yet set; a mark could show as a large square for a frame. Loading a new area is not delayed. The ticks are counted by the plugin itself, as the client's tick count restarts at login.
- Quest Helper's tile style follows walking NPCs in the scene: their tile lies between tiles, which matched no scene tile, so Quest Helper drew it in 2D until the NPC stood still. Such tiles are matched to the NPC (NPC.getCanvasTilePoly) and drawn where the NPC is each frame.
- New option "Extend plugin ranges" (off by default): Agility, NPC Aggression Timer, Blast Furnace and Pyramid Plunder marks as far as the draw distance instead of those plugins' own limits (about 18 and 20 tiles).
- Tile Packs: packs made by the player were not drawn with Tile Packs 2, which saves each pack under its own key. Both its current and its earlier format are read.
- Many marked NPCs (a field of cows with every style) no longer slow down or flicker between outline and hull: outline tracing is about twice as fast with the same result (the raster kept as bits, edges linked in flat arrays instead of a hash map), and up to 256 hulls, clickboxes and outlines are drawn in the scene instead of 64; beyond the limit they fell back to RuneLite's much costlier 2D outline renderer.
- Performance: fewer allocations per frame and per tick, and Tile Packs' tiles and its held overlay switch in the same tick.
- Shortest Path: path tiles behind a hill or other raised terrain disappeared. A captured tile polygon was matched by a ray from the camera, which stopped at the terrain in front; tiles are now matched exactly against the neighbours of the previous path tile, or else every scene tile's projected centre, so nothing in between matters.
- GPU plugin: marks drawn through walls were all invisible when the through-walls object hung at the camera. The GPU plugin skips a see-through model whole if any vertex is nearer the camera than 50; unused vertices sat at the object's anchor. They now sit on a drawn vertex, and pulled vertices keep a margin.
- 117 HD: light colours (such as pink) no longer turn into their pure colour. 117 HD's default shading caps the lightness of coloured faces; such colours are now drawn as the capped colour with white over it, blending to the original colour.
- Shortest Path: its path tiles and lines are drawn in the scene. HD Tile Markers runs its path overlay once per frame and turns the tiles and lines it draws into scene marks; its tile counters and transport hints, and any shape that matches no tile, stay 2D. With its transport or collision map debug overlays on, or after repeated errors in its overlay, its own overlay stays.
- Through-walls marks are drawn after see-through scenery such as tree leaves, so their fill no longer disappears at some camera angles.
- A chat notice when 117 HD's "Shadow transparency" is on, shown again after turning the plugin on.
- Through-walls faces stay under 117 HD's shadow threshold when its shadow transparency is off.
- Fixes: scene geometry read by the renderer while the next frame is written, the carrier model being reloaded every frame after a failed load, and a possible out-of-bounds read in the hover path.
- Replaced deprecated RuneLite API calls in the Path Marker, Better NPC Highlight and Gauntlet integrations.

## 0.1.2

Use the original development preset as the plugin defaults, including tile colors, corner markers, path colors and draw distance. Existing saved settings retain precedence.

## 0.1.1

- Enforce the outline raster cell limit including padding; reject non-finite, oversized and excessively overlapping projections before raster allocation/work.
- Fall back per marker when scene rendering skips a model or tile, including the 64-model limit; preserve native NPC/Object outline settings and Better NPC Highlight style rendering.
- Keep offscreen and transparent markers handled without drawing them twice.
- Restore held overlays only when their owning plugin and feature are still enabled.

## 0.1.0

Initial release:

- Scene-rendered tile, NPC, object and walking-path markers, including Stretched Mode support and 2D fallback.
- Rendering integrations for Ground Markers, Object Markers, NPC Indicators, Better NPC Highlight, Tile Packs, Stealing Artefacts, Sailing and NPC Aggression Timer.
- Walking-path estimates respect display keybinds, Never, minimap-only and target-only settings.
- External plugin markers clear across session/profile changes and shutdown.
